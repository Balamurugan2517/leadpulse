LeadPulse AI — End-to-End User Guide

Welcome to LeadPulse AI, your B2B lead intelligence, technical website audit, and client outreach platform built for solo digital agency founders.

1. Command Centre (/dashboard)

Provides an immediate daily overview of your agency pipeline:

  • Actively Looking (Tier 1): Prospects with explicit buying intent or RFP signals.
  • High Demand Gaps (Tier 2): Businesses missing a website or suffering from expired SSL / mobile issues.
  • Outreach Pending: Ready-to-send WhatsApp and Email drafts awaiting founder review.
  • Pipeline Value: Active deal value in INR across your sales stages.

2. Discovery Studio (/dashboard/discovery)

Discover targeted B2B prospects across India with zero mandatory paid subscriptions:

  1. Target Location (PAN India): Select State/UT → District/City → Locality/Area.
  2. Industry & Radius: Pick your target vertical (Clinics, Tuition, Restaurants, CA/Legal, Salons & Spas) and search radius (3 km, 5 km, or 10 km).
  3. Lead Source & Limit: Choose OpenStreetMap (Overpass), Startup India & MSME Registry, or All Free Sources, and set a scan limit (10 to 1000 leads).
  4. Run Discovery Scan: Watch the 4-phase live progress bar. Results appear in a scrollable staging list with automatic deduplication.
  5. Save to CRM: Click + Save to CRM on individual leads or Import All to CRM to persist unique prospects to Neon PostgreSQL.
  6. Quick-Capture Bookmarklet: Drag the bookmarklet button to your browser bar. While viewing any business on Google Maps or Instagram, click it to import the business in 1 click.

3. Lead Explorer (/dashboard/leads)

  • Search & Filter: Filter by Demand Tier (Actively Looking, High Demand, Medium) or search by name, phone, or category.
  • Google Maps Geolocation: Open Details on any lead and click Locate on Google Maps to view exact address and coordinates.
  • Delete Unwanted Leads: Click Delete (twice to confirm) in the Actions column or inside Lead Details to permanently remove unwanted leads.
  • Quick CSV Import: Paste CSV rows at the bottom of Lead Explorer; formula injection protection and deduplication run automatically.

4. Website Audit Lab (/dashboard/audit-lab)

  • Enter any target website URL and click Run Technical Audit.
  • Checks SSL certificate validity/expiry, mobile viewport responsiveness, HTTP status, TTFB latency, and missing WhatsApp/contact CTAs.
  • Completed audits automatically appear in Recent Website Diagnostics and can be exported anytime using Download CSV Report.

5. Live Client Prototypes (/preview/[slug]) & Outreach Desk

Each prospect receives an instant, niche-tailored website preview with industry-specific layout, color theme, photography, and 1-click WhatsApp booking. Share this link directly from Outreach Desk via 1-click WhatsApp or Email.

6. Sales Pipeline & Proposals (/dashboard/pipeline)

Move deals across Kanban stages (Discovered → Contacted → In Discussion → Proposal Sent → Closed Won), log meeting notes, and generate printable client proposals.
